Missed follow-ups are a patient safety issue first. They are also a financial one. The model below traces how recommended follow-up imaging that is never completed turns into lost revenue, and what recovery would look like for a 30-radiologist practice.

The Revenue You're Leaving on the Table

Missed follow-ups aren't just a patient safety issue — they're a financial one

1

11–20% of CT and MR studies contain follow-up recommendations

That's thousands of recommended follow-up exams per year across your practice.

2

~42% of those are never completed

Patients who don't understand their reports don't come back. That's lost imaging revenue and unmanaged liability risk.

3

Research suggests significant recovery is achievable

Published studies show that structured, actionable recommendations can improve follow-up compliance by up to 29 percentage points — potentially recovering substantial lost revenue while improving patient outcomes.

What the research shows

42% of recommended follow-ups are never completed. In a study of ~3 million radiology exams, Mabotuwana et al. found an overall adherence rate of just 58%.
Mabotuwana T, et al. J Am Coll Radiol. 2018;15(3 Pt A):422–428.

A 2022 JAMA Network Open study at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center demonstrated that structured, actionable recommendations pushed compliance from ~58% to 87.4% — a 29-percentage-point improvement.
White T, Aronson MD, Sternberg SB, et al. JAMA Netw Open. 2022;5(7):e2222549.

ImpressionAssist is designed to build on this approach — combining patient-readable language with explicit action items to help patients understand what they need to do and why it matters.
