Research published in the American Journal of Roentgenology found that patients understand only 50% of their radiology reports, with 80% reporting little or no understanding of CT reports. Meanwhile, studies show that approximately 42% of radiology follow-up recommendations are never completed — leading to delayed diagnoses, patient harm, and significant lost revenue. ImpressionAssist is built to help close both gaps with a single click.
